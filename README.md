One line roasting an alarm clock that rings at 6 AM on a Saturday, entered in the [Snooze Button Roast bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-carbon-forge-snooze-button-roast-14cbafc6) on make.fun.

## The line

> My alarm clock rang at 6 AM on a Saturday like a debt collector who knows I am home, except the only thing I owe it is eight more hours of sleep.

## How it works

`main.py` is stdlib only. It writes the line above to `/output/entry.txt`. No network, no randomness, no input. Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
